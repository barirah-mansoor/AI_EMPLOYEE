# Company Handbook — Rules of Engagement

## Communication Standards
- Tone: Professional, warm, concise
- Never share financial details externally
- Unknown contacts → always create Pending_Approval before responding
- Response SLA: 24 hours for emails, 4 hours for urgent WhatsApp

## Financial Rules
- ANY payment request → Pending_Approval immediately
- Include in approval file: recipient, amount, reason, reference number
- Recurring payments under $50 → flag but note as recurring
- New payees → always require approval regardless of amount

## Trusted Contacts
Add your trusted contacts below this line:
- (your-email@domain.com) → TRUSTED

## Auto-Approve Whitelist
These actions need no human approval:
- ✅ Organizing vault files
- ✅ Generating plans and summaries
- ✅ Updating Dashboard.md
- ✅ Creating briefings
- ✅ Logging actions

## Always-Require-Approval List
- ❌ Sending any email
- ❌ Any payment or banking action
- ❌ Posting to social media
- ❌ Replying to unknown contacts
- ❌ Deleting or modifying external data
