# Company Handbook — Rules of Engagement

## Communication Standards
- Tone: Professional, warm, concise
- Never share financial details externally
- Unknown contacts → always create Pending_Approval before responding
- Response SLA: 24 hours for emails, 4 hours for urgent WhatsApp

## Financial Rules
- ANY payment request → Pending_Approval immediately
- Include in approval file: recipient, amount, reason, reference number
- Recurring payments under $50 → flag but note as recurring
- New payees → always require approval regardless of amount

## Trusted Contacts
Add your trusted contacts below this line:
- (your-email@domain.com) → TRUSTED

## Auto-Approve Whitelist
These actions need no human approval:
- ✅ Organizing vault files
- ✅ Generating plans and summaries
- ✅ Updating Dashboard.md
- ✅ Creating briefings
- ✅ Logging actions

## Always-Require-Approval List
- ❌ Sending any email
- ❌ Any payment or banking action
- ❌ Posting to social media
- ❌ Replying to unknown contacts
- ❌ Deleting or modifying external data

## 🥈 Silver Tier Email Processing Protocol

### Trusted Contact Categories

**Internal Team (Auto-acknowledge allowed)**
- Colleagues with company email domain
- Regular project collaborators
- Internal system notifications

**Clients & Partners (Draft for approval)**
- Paying customers
- Business partners
- Vendor communications
- Contract-related emails

**Financial & Legal (Always escalate)**
- Invoices, payments, billing
- Legal documents or requests
- Compliance communications
- Audit or regulatory inquiries

**Unknown Senders (Flag for review)**
- First-time contacts
- External sales pitches
- Media inquiries
- Suspicious or phishing attempts

### Auto-Response Templates

**Meeting Acknowledgment:**
"Thank you for the meeting invitation. I'm checking availability and will respond within 2 hours."

**Information Request:**
"I've received your request for information. I'm gathering the details and will have a complete response within 24 hours."

**General Inquiry:**
"Thank you for contacting us. Your message is important and will be reviewed promptly. Expected response time: 24-48 hours."

**Support Request:**
"Your support request has been logged with ID: [AUTO_GENERATED]. Our team will investigate and respond within one business day."

### Escalation Triggers (Always require human approval)

**Security Concerns:**
- Password reset requests
- Account access issues
- Suspicious login notifications
- Security vulnerability reports

**Financial Matters:**
- Payment requests or disputes
- Budget discussions
- Contract negotiations
- Expense approvals over $100

**Sensitive Communications:**
- Performance reviews or HR matters
- Confidential project information
- Customer complaints or escalations
- Legal or compliance issues

### Response Time Guidelines

| Priority | Auto-Acknowledgment | Full Response Target |
|----------|---------------------|----------------------|
| RED (Urgent) | Immediate | 2 hours |
| YELLOW (High) | 1 hour | 24 hours |
| GREEN (Normal) | 4 hours | 48 hours |
